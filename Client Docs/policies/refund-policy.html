At Yours Waggly, we want you and your pet to love every purchase. If something isn't right, this policy explains how cancellations, returns, replacements, and refunds work.

1. Cancellations

You may request to cancel an order before it has been dispatched. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but you may be able to return eligible items after delivery (see below). To cancel, contact us as soon as possible with your order number.

2. Return Window

You may request a return within [7] days of delivery, provided the item meets the eligibility conditions below.

3. Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, an item must be:

  • Unused, unworn, and in the same condition you received it.
  • In its original packaging with all tags and seals intact.
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt).

4. Non-Returnable Items

For the safety and hygiene of pets, some items cannot be returned once opened or used, including:

  • Food, treats, and other perishable or consumable products.
  • Opened grooming, health, or hygiene products.
  • Personalised or custom-made items.
  • Clearance or final-sale items (where marked).

5. Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us within [48 hours] of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no extra cost to you.

6. How to Start a Return or Replacement

  1. Contact us using the details below with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Our team will confirm eligibility and share return instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it to the address we provide.

Please do not send items back before receiving confirmation from us.

7. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the outcome. Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within [7–10 business days]. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take additional time for the amount to reflect.

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (for example, a damaged, defective, or wrong item).

8. Return Shipping Costs

Unless the return is due to our error, return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer. We recommend using a trackable shipping method, as we cannot be responsible for items lost in transit.

9. Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received an approved refund within the stated timeline, please check with your bank or payment provider first, then contact us and we'll help.
